## Voice standard

The edition should read like engineering nonfiction written in English from the start:

- short paragraphs when the idea deserves emphasis;
- concrete examples before abstractions when possible;
- explicit distinctions such as capability vs. reliability and model vs. system;
- no fake certainty, hype, anthropomorphism, or motivational filler;
- technical terms preserved when they are the normal industry vocabulary;
- evidence and verification preferred over persuasive prose.
