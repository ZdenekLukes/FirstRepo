# English Edition Notes — v0.8-eng

## What This Edition Is

`book_final_eng/` is an international English adaptation of the Czech print-candidate master in `book_final/`.

It is intentionally **not a literal translation**. The Czech edition is the structural and factual source, but the English text is re-authored so it reads as native technical nonfiction.

## Adaptation Decisions

- chapter titles are changed where a more natural English title is stronger;
- explanatory prose is generally tighter;
- repeated transitional sentences are reduced;
- engineering distinctions, equations, tables, checklists, and failure modes are preserved;
- Czech-only linguistic examples are generalized into multilingual engineering lessons;
- EU AI Act / GDPR remain as a concrete European jurisdiction case inside a broader global security/governance model;
- the analog IC design case study remains intact because it is technically distinctive and globally understandable;
- personal hardware experiments remain personal observations and are not converted into universal benchmark claims.

## Intended Reader

The book should work for two readers at once:

1. a technically minded beginner who wants to understand modern AI without unnecessary mathematics;
2. an experienced practitioner who wants a durable engineering model connecting LLMs, RAG, tools, agents, security, evals, and economics.

The book should never become shallow in order to become accessible.

## International Positioning

Working title:

> **AI: From First Principles to Agentic Systems**

Working subtitle:

> **How AI Actually Works — and How to Build Reliable Systems with Models, Data, Tools, and Verification**

The positioning deliberately avoids “100 prompts,” “AI hacks,” or a vendor/product catalog. The durable subject is the system around the model.

## Snapshot Policy

The English edition inherits the Czech master’s factual snapshot date of **August 7, 2026** for fast-moving model, tool, hardware, and regulatory material.

Before an actual international publication, perform a fresh primary-source check of all snapshot sections and update the edition date consistently rather than patching individual product names ad hoc.

## English Editorial Rule

If a passage sounds like a translation, rewrite it.

If a passage sounds impressive but cannot survive the question “how do we verify that?”, rewrite it.

If a visual does not compress a useful idea, remove it.

## Publication Ambition

The quality bar is an internationally useful practical AI book that can stand beside serious technical nonfiction, not a promise of bestseller status. Bestseller outcomes depend on editorial quality **plus** distribution, timing, positioning, reviews, community, and marketing.

The manuscript should therefore optimize for the part we control: clarity, depth, usefulness, intellectual honesty, memorable mental models, and technical credibility.
